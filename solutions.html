Medical Follow-Up & Reactivation Loop

Most clinics lose patients not to competition, but to silence. A patient completes three sessions of ten, starts feeling better, and disappears. A lapsed patient receives nothing for 90 days and books somewhere else. This system closes both gaps automatically, keeping active patients on track and bringing past patients back.

Live in 1–2 weeks
Get a demo →
What changes
  • ✦8 to 15% of lapsed patients rebook within 30 days, contacted automatically at 60, 90, and 180 days of inactivity
  • ✦Treatment plan dropout stops. Patients are nudged to book their next session before they quietly stop coming
  • ✦Sentiment-branching follow-ups. Happy patients get a review request, dissatisfied ones alert your team immediately
  • ✦Referral pipeline activated, with the ask sent two weeks after a successful outcome, when the goodwill is still fresh
  • ✦Connects directly to Jane App and Cliniko, so there is no manual data entry and no new software to learn
